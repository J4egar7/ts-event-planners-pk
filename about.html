The studio

Three things, done properly

Venue, décor, catering. It sounds simple until you're three weeks out with a guest list that grew by eighty people and a hall that can't take another table.

Our job is to be the people who already thought about that. We shortlist the venue, design the room, plan the food and hold the schedule — and we stay reachable from the first meeting to the morning after, when the last thing has been loaded out.

Most of our clients come from families and companies in Karachi who've seen our work at someone else's function. That's still how it works here: one good evening leads to the next booking.

How we work

The plan is written down, not remembered

Every event we take has a document behind it: what's booked, who arrives when, what happens at each point in the evening, and who is responsible for it.

That document is why a function can absorb a late chief guest, a changed menu or a photographer who needs ten more minutes — without the family having to solve it in the moment.

  • One contactYou deal with one coordinator, not one number per vendor.
  • Written scopeWhat's included, what isn't, and what it costs — before anything is booked.
  • Site visitThe room is checked in person, with the layout drawn to its real dimensions.
  • On-site teamPresent from the first load-in to the last chair going back on the truck.
