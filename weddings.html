Mehndi · nikkah · valima

The wedding your family keeps talking about

Three functions, three moods, one plan. We handle the venue, the décor, the food and the running order so that on the day your family is sitting with guests instead of chasing vendors.

The three functions

Each night has its own job to do

The mistake most weddings make is treating all three functions as the same party with a different outfit. They're not. Mehndi is loud, nikkah is formal, valima is the reception — and each one needs a different room, sound level and menu.

Mehndi

Colour and movement. A stage the cousins can dance in front of, marigold and mirror work, a dhol that's actually audible, and food that works standing up.

Nikkah

The quiet one. Family seating that respects who sits where, a clean backdrop for the photographs, and a ceremony that starts when it was written down to start.

Valima

The reception. A stage built for the entrance and the photographs, a dinner service that keeps the queue short, and a hall that still looks full at midnight.

01 · Décor & stage

The stage is the photograph everyone keeps

We design the stage around the room, not around a catalogue. That means checking the ceiling height, the sightlines from the back rows, where the entrance will be filmed from, and which colours survive under the venue's lighting rather than in daylight.

  • Stage, backdrop and entrance design drawn to the hall's dimensions
  • Fresh florals, drapes and table styling
  • Lighting that flatters skin tones and doesn't wash out the décor
  • Photo areas guests actually use, not a corner nobody finds
02 · Venue

The room decides half the budget

A hall that holds 400 comfortably will look empty at 250, and a lawn that's perfect in November is a different venue in June. We visit venues with you, in daylight and where possible during a live event, and tell you what each one is actually like to work in.

  • Hall, lawn, marquee and hotel ballroom options
  • Capacity checked against your real guest list
  • Parking, access, generator and load-in checked before booking
  • Floor plans for seating, stage, buffet and the dance floor
03 · Catering

Food that reaches the table hot

The menu is planned around the hour, the crowd and the venue's kitchen — not just the dish list. A seated dinner for 300 needs a different plan from a mehndi buffet where people eat standing and keep dancing.

  • Menu planning and a tasting before you commit
  • Buffet, family-style or plated service
  • Canapés and welcome drinks for the arrival hour
  • Service staff numbers matched to the guest count
04 · Coordination

Someone else holding the schedule

On the day, we run the programme: vendor arrivals, the stage setup, the family photographs, the entrances and the dinner call. Your family gets a single number to call, and it isn't the caterer's.

  • A written run-of-show shared with the family beforehand
  • Vendor load-in, setup and load-out managed on site
  • Guest seating and family protocol handled discreetly
  • Our team present from the first truck to the last chair

Before you call

Four things that make the first meeting useful

You don't need a finished plan to call us — but these four answers turn a conversation into an actual quote.

Do you work outside DHA and outside Karachi?

Karachi is home ground and most of our work is here. For events elsewhere in Sindh or up-country, travel and transport are built into the quote — tell us the city on the first call and we'll say honestly whether it's practical.

Can we book you for just décor, or just coordination?

Yes. Some families only want the stage and the entrance done properly; others have the décor sorted and need someone to run the day. Full planning is usually better value because the vendors and the schedule are handled together, but single-service bookings are welcome.

How far ahead should we book?

Wedding season and December fill up first — for a valima in peak season, three to four months is comfortable. Off-season and smaller functions can often be arranged in a few weeks. The date is the one thing that can't be fixed later.

Do you handle the photography and video?

We coordinate the photography and video team as part of the plan and schedule their timings around the stage, the entrances and the family photographs, so nobody is standing in the wrong place at the wrong moment.

What do you need from us?

A date, a city or area, a rough guest count, and a budget range. If you already have a venue or a décor idea, bring it — we'll tell you what will work in that room.
