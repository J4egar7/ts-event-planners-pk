What we actually do

Planning is a thousand small decisions. We take most of them off you.

Most families come to us with a date, a rough guest count and a feeling about how the evening should look. We turn that into a venue shortlist, a décor scheme, a menu, a floor plan and a running order — and then we stand in the room on the day and make it happen in that order.

The corporate side is the same discipline with a different brief: an AGM has to start on time, a launch has to land on the cue, and a dinner has to seat three hundred people without a queue at the door.

Weddings

Three functions, one thread

Mehndi, nikkah and valima each have their own mood and their own logistics. We plan them as one programme so the colours, the food and the guest list don't repeat themselves.

Mehndi

Colour, dhol and a stage built for dancing — marigold, mirrors and lighting that keeps the floor bright.

Nikkah

A quieter room, a proper seating plan for the families, and a ceremony that runs to time without being rushed.

Valima

The reception: a stage that photographs beautifully, a dinner service that doesn't queue, and a clear exit at the end.

How it runs

Four stages, and you always know where you are

The brief

Date, city, guest count, budget range and the mood you're after. One sitting, usually over chai, is enough to start.

The plan

Venue options, a décor direction, a menu and a floor plan — costed line by line so you can cut what you don't want.

The build

Vendors booked, load-in scheduled, and a walkthrough in the actual room before anything is finalised.

The day

Our team on site from setup to load-out, running the programme from a cue sheet your family never has to read.
