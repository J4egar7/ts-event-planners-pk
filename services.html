Venue · décor · catering · coordination

One team for the whole evening

You can hire us for the stage alone or for everything from the venue shortlist to the last chair going back on the truck. Most clients start with the full plan and cut back once they see the numbers.

01 · Venue

Finding the room

We start with the guest count and the date, then shortlist halls, lawns, marquees and hotel ballrooms that actually fit — and visit them with you rather than sending a brochure. Capacity, parking, generator, kitchen access and load-in all get checked before you pay a deposit.

  • Shortlist built around headcount, date and budget
  • Site visits in daylight and during live events where possible
  • Floor plans for stage, seating, buffet and dance floor
  • Contract and payment terms explained before you sign
02 · Décor

Stage, florals and the entrance

Décor is designed for the room and the camera: the height of the ceiling, the colour of the light, the angle the entrance will be filmed from. We build the scheme in drawings first, so nothing is decided on the morning of the event.

  • Mehndi, nikkah, valima and corporate stage design
  • Fresh florals, drapes, mirrors and props
  • Table styling, centrepieces and place settings
  • Entrance, photo area and signage
03 · Catering

Menus planned around the hour

The food has to suit the function: a mehndi crowd eats standing and keeps dancing, a valima is a seated dinner with a service curve, a conference lunch has thirty minutes and three hundred people. We plan the menu and the service together.

  • Menu planning and tasting
  • Buffet, family-style and plated service
  • Canapés, welcome drinks and late-night servings
  • Service staff and timings written into the run-of-show
04 · Guests

Lists, seating and the family politics

Seating is the part guests notice and families worry about. We keep the list, build the table plan, mark the reserved rows and manage the arrival desk — including the relatives who arrive without an invitation.

  • Guest list consolidation and RSVP follow-up
  • Table plan and place cards
  • Reserved seating for family and VIPs
  • Arrival and registration desk on the day
05 · Coordination

The day itself

Vendors arrive at different times with different ideas about when they finish. Someone has to hold the schedule. Our team is on site from the first load-in to the final load-out, running the programme from a written cue sheet.

  • Run-of-show agreed with your family or your team
  • Vendor arrivals, setup and load-out supervised
  • Timings called for entrances, speeches and dinner
  • One number for everyone to call instead of five
06 · Corporate

Conferences, launches and company events

Corporate work is a schedule problem before it's a design problem. We plan the room, the registration, the breaks and the catering around the parts of the programme that cannot move — and coordinate the AV and staging suppliers with your own team.

  • Conferences, AGMs, seminars and dealer meets
  • Product launches and reveal cues
  • Award nights and annual dinners
  • Government and institutional events

How a quote is built

Line by line, so you can cut what you don't want

We don't quote a single lump sum for "a wedding". You get a breakdown — venue, décor, catering, staffing, coordination — with what each part includes. If the total is above your budget, you can see exactly where to trim instead of guessing.

Nothing is booked before you approve the breakdown, and nothing is added later without asking you first.

Questions

Straight answers

Do you only work in Karachi?

Karachi is where most of our work is, and where we can be on site quickly. For events in other cities we'll quote travel and transport openly — sometimes it makes sense, sometimes it doesn't, and we'll say so.

Can you work with our own caterer or decorator?

Yes. Plenty of families have a caterer they trust or a decorator they've used before. We'll coordinate them into the plan and the schedule instead of replacing them.

Do you take on small events?

Yes — engagements, birthdays, anniversaries and dinners at home. The scope is smaller but the planning is the same: seating, food, timing and someone on site.

Who is on site on the day?

Our own coordinator and support team, from setup through to load-out. You'll have their number before the event and they'll have yours.

How do payments work?

A booking amount to hold the date, a payment once the vendors are confirmed, and the balance before the event. The schedule is written into the quote so there are no surprises.
